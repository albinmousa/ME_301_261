Subscribe, don't import. Use the Subscribe button rather than downloading the file. A downloaded copy is frozen at today's schedule; a subscription keeps pulling the latest version, so any change to the plan reaches you automatically.

How to subscribe

  • Google Calendar — use the “Add to Google Calendar” button, or: Other calendars → From URL → paste the link below.
  • Outlook / Microsoft 365 — Add calendar → Subscribe from web → paste the link below.
  • Apple Calendar (Mac / iPhone) — the Subscribe button opens the dialog directly. On Mac you can then set Refresh to hourly.

How quickly updates reach you. Calendar apps refresh subscriptions on their own schedule — Google and Outlook typically every few hours up to about a day; Apple Calendar lets you choose. So a schedule change may take a little while to appear. For anything time-critical, the announcement in class and on Blackboard is the authority.
